**Pua**

a mix of pong and lua... with my own special twists! all for the sake of learning about lua + a bit about game design :)

9/9/2025

it's done! after much procrastination, here is the final product: https://kamero0n.itch.io/pong
it's not too much, but i feel happy that i can conclude it, without it haunting the back of my brain... even if the concept was originally for me to just practice lua. but now i can move on to other projects -- yippee!!

mostly everything is made by me except for the shader (although it has a few modifications). the shader comes from this forum: https://love2d.org/forums/viewtopic.php?t=85736
it's a crt shader that i liked, and wanted to have a more "retro-y" effect.

overall it's a nice way to warm up with lua, and i feel pretty easy-going w/ love2d (although there's def more i could learn about).
